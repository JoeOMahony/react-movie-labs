https://api.themoviedb.org/3/search/movie?api_key=APIKEYGOESHERE&query=QUERYGOESHERE&year=YEARGOESHERE

THEN,
https://api.themoviedb.org/3/movie/MOVIEIDGOESHERE?api_key=APIKEYGOESHERE

https://api.themoviedb.org/3/trending/person/week?api_key=APIKEYGOESHERE&language=en-US
